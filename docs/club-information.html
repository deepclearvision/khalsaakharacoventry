About Us

The Home of Kushti

Khalsa Akhara Wrestling Coventry is the city’s home of traditional Punjabi wrestling.

Kushti is the ancient art of the akhara — wrestling built on strength, technique and character. At Khalsa Akhara we carry that tradition into every session, for every wrestler.

We welcome wrestlers of every age and experience — from first-timers learning to move on the mat, to competitors chasing medals on the national stage. Every session is coached with discipline and heart, and every member, from our youngest wrestler to our veterans, is held to the same standard.

Our akhara is built on the warrior values of the Khalsa: honesty, integrity, respect, empathy, kindness, humility and compassion. There is zero tolerance for bullying or discrimination of any kind — the mat is a place where everyone belongs.

Why families choose us — at a glance

Coached sessions every week • beginner-friendly at every age • a safe, zero-tolerance environment • mats cleaned after every session • a clear pathway from first steps to tournament competition • and a community that supports each other, on and off the mat.

Why Wrestle

The Benefits of Wrestling

Wrestling is one of the oldest sports on earth — and one of the best things a young person can do.

Full-body fitnessStrength, speed, flexibility and stamina — wrestling develops every part of the body.
Discipline & focusTechnique rewards patience and repetition. Wrestlers learn to concentrate and work at a goal.
ConfidenceEvery session proves what you’re made of. Confidence built on the mat lasts a lifetime.
Respect & humilityWin with humility, lose with grace. Wrestling teaches respect for opponents, coaches and yourself.
Friendship & communityTeammates become family. The akhara is a community that supports each other in everything.
A competitive pathwayFor wrestlers who want to test themselves, we compete at events across the country.
