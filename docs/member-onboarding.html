Welcome

Welcome to the Akhara

Khalsa Akhara Wrestling Coventry is the city’s home of traditional Punjabi wrestling — kushti — the ancient art of the akhara.

Whether you’re stepping onto the mat for the very first time or joining us from another club, you are joining a family. We welcome wrestlers of every age and ability, and every session is coached with discipline and heart.

Our akhara is built on the warrior values of the Khalsa — honesty, integrity, respect, empathy, kindness, humility and compassion. You’ll hear these words a lot, because on our mats they are not just words: they are how we train, how we compete, and how we treat each other.

There is zero tolerance for bullying or discrimination of any kind. The mat is a place where everyone belongs.

Your first session — what to expect

Arrive a few minutes early to settle in and meet the coaches. Sessions usually follow the same shape: a warm-up, technique work, drilling and live wrestling. Wear comfortable sports clothing with no zips, buttons or pockets — and bring a bottle of water. Parents and guardians are welcome to watch from the side of the mat.

The Rules of the Akhara

General Rules

A few simple rules keep the mat safe, clean and respectful for everyone.

  1. No shoes on the mat — ever.Outdoor footwear stays outside the wrestling area. Mats are cleaned and disinfected after every single session; a clean mat is a safe mat.
  2. No food inside the training area.Eat before or after the session, never on the mats. Water bottles are welcome.
  3. Wear the right kit.Comfortable sports clothing with no zips, buttons or sharp edges. No jewellery or watches on the mat.
  4. Follow the coach — first time, every time.Coaches keep everyone safe. Instructions are for your safety as much as your progress.
  5. Look after the venue.Leave the akhara as you found it — tidy kit, rubbish in the bin, everything respected.
  6. Support from the sidelines.Parents and guardians cheer everyone on — coaching is left to the coaches.

Behaviour — on and off the mat

Treat everyone with respect — coaches, teammates, opponents and visitors. Win with humility, lose with grace, and always shake hands. No swearing, no bullying, no bad attitudes. The way you behave in the akhara is the way you represent the club everywhere else.

Safety First

Safety & Welfare

Wrestling is a contact sport — these rules keep every wrestler safe, healthy and able to train at their best.

  • Kakars are securely covered at all times.Kakars must be covered securely and safely before stepping onto the mat — for your protection and everyone else’s.
  • Infections and open wounds — stay home.Do not train with any infection or open wound. Wrestlers must stay off the mat until fully healed and cleared to return.
  • Keep nails short.Short fingernails and toenails prevent cuts and scratches for you and your training partners.
  • Report every injury straight away.Even a small knock. Coaches carry first-aid support at every session and need to know immediately.
  • Warm up properly.Never skip the warm-up — it’s the foundation of every safe session.

Attendance — the 75% ethos

Wrestlers commit to 75% attendance. Excellence on the mat is built on consistency — every session missed is progress missed, so commit to showing up.

Competitions — the squad pathway

Competition places are earned through attendance, attitude and progress. Coaches select the squad for each event — and every wrestler, at every level, gets a clear path onto the tournament mat.
