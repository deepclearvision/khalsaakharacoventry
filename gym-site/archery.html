About the Club

A Sport for
Everyone

Khalsa Archers Coventry

Khalsa Akhara Archery Club — Khalsa Archers Coventry — brings archery to the gym every Tuesday at 6pm, on the archery range at Khalsa Akhara Gym.

The club’s message is simple: “Archery really is a sport for everyone.” It’s a sport where age, size and background don’t decide who succeeds — focus and practice do.

Every Tuesday at 6pm All ages Beginners welcome

Age 9 to 64

In one recent week, the club’s star archers were Mr Singh, aged 64 — and Simar Kaur, aged 9. Same club, same sport, same smiles.

That’s the club in a single week: grandparents and grandchildren learning side by side, all representing Khalsa Akhara at tournaments.

What We Offer

From First Arrow to
Tournament Floor

Coached Sessions

Weekly coached sessions covering stance, draw, aim and release — with safety first at every step.

Equipment Guidance

New to the sport? The club will guide you on bows, arrows and kit — no need to buy anything before your first session.

Representing the Gym

Khalsa Archers compete in tournaments as part of Khalsa Akhara Gym — wearing the akhara colours with pride.

Focus & Patience

Archery is meditation with a target. Every arrow is a lesson in breathing, stillness and calm under pressure — skills that carry into school, work and life.

The Akhara Spirit

Archery sits deep in the Sikh martial tradition — from the great archers of the Gurus’ time to the sport practised at the gym today. Training the body, steadying the mind.

Take Your
First Shot

Sessions every Tuesday at 6pm at Khalsa Akhara Gym, Cross Road, Coventry. Contact us to join in — everyone is welcome.
