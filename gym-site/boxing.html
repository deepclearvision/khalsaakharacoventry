About the Club

Built on Years of
Hard Work

England Boxing Affiliated

In January 2025, Khalsa Akhara Boxing proudly announced its affiliation to England Boxing — the national governing body for amateur boxing.

The club is registered as Champions Corner ABC, which provides excellent opportunities for all our boxers and opens up boxing at national and European levels.

England Boxing Champions Corner ABC National & European pathway

A Club Built Together

In the club’s own words: “It’s been a long journey of hard work and commitment from many different people over the years — this brings us to a new chapter of our combat journey.”

The boxing club was started with the help of Mani Singh, and grew through the hard work and commitment of Bhai Tarsem Singh and Dalbara Singh — the people who set the club up all those years ago.

Coaching

In Your Corner

Experienced coaches with deep roots in Sikh community boxing — and a coaching culture built on discipline and respect.

Indi Singh

Boxing Coach

Indi Singh runs the Khalsa Akhara Boxing Club and coaches for Sikh Ethos — the Sikh combat-sports organisation with 11 boxing clubs across England. Under his coaching, the club has grown from grassroots sessions to England Boxing affiliation and competitive bout cards.

Mani Sandhu

Founder & Coach

Mani has lived combat sport his whole life, starting with an excellent amateur boxing career before moving into MMA and wrestling. He was one of the people who helped start the boxing club — and he is still at the heart of coaching at the gym today.

Sikh Ethos

Khalsa Akhara boxing coaches work alongside Sikh Ethos, the Sikh combat-sports organisation — a network of 11 boxing clubs across England keeping Sikh athletes in the ring while staying true to their faith.

That campaign made national news in 2018, when England Boxing lifted its ban on bearded amateur boxers — a change long championed by Sikh coaches and boxers, opening the sport to observant Sikhs while remaining safe for everyone.

Faith & sport together Sikh Ethos network Beard rule lifted 2018

Training

What to Expect

Fundamentals First

Stance, footwork, guard and straight shots — every boxer builds from the ground up, whatever their starting point.

Fitness & Discipline

Boxing conditioning is second to none — and the club’s standards are built on the gym’s no-excuses attitude.

A Pathway to Compete

Through Champions Corner ABC and England Boxing, committed boxers can step up to competitive bouts — and beyond.
