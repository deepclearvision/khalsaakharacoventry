What Is Kushti?

Kushti is the traditional wrestling of the Punjab — one of the oldest living martial arts in the world. For centuries it was taught in the akhara, the earthen training pit that gave the sport its discipline, its diet and its soul.

Today the art lives on in modern Sikh wrestling clubs across the UK — and at Khalsa Akhara, the club wrestles on regulation mats with traditional values at its heart.

From the Gurus to the Mat

The Sikh Gurus championed the ideal of the sant-sipahi — the saint-soldier who trains strength and humility together. Wrestling has been part of Sikh tradition since the time of the Gurus, and Punjab has produced some of the greatest wrestlers who ever lived.

When you step onto the mat at Khalsa Akhara Wrestling Coventry, you step into a living tradition.
