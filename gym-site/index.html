About the Gym ਜਿਮ ਬਾਰੇ

Strength for the
Whole Community

Opened in
Coventry 2013

Khalsa Akhara Gym Coventry provides state-of-the-art training facilities for the Sikh community in and around Coventry — and welcomes everyone who shares our values.

Opened in 2013, the gym was officially opened with the ribbon cut by the legendary Bhai Fauja Singh — the world’s oldest marathon runner. His example still sets the tone: no matter your age or starting point, hard work gets you further than excuses.

From free weights to a huge selection of machines, showers, sauna, gymnasium and all modern amenities at one location — everything you need is under one roof. And under that same roof, three clubs train every week: wrestling, boxing and archery.

Sikh Heritage ਵਿਰਾਸਤ

A Warrior Tradition,
Alive in Coventry

The Sikh community has a rich history in the martial arts — and the akhara has always been where that tradition lives. This is the inheritance our gym carries forward.

  1. The Ancient Akhara

    An akhara is a traditional training ground built around an earthen wrestling pit. For over a thousand years these gymnasiums trained the body, steadied the mind and held communities together — “a shrine of strength where earth is turned into gold”.

  2. The Gurus & the Warrior Ideal

    The Sikh Gurus made the akhara a place where a strong body and a strong mind were trained side by side — the sant-sipahi, the saint-soldier. Martial arts like gatka and shastar vidya grew from that same tradition of disciplined self-defence.

  3. Legends of the Mat

    Punjab’s wrestling greats — the undefeated Great Gama, Dara Singh, Kartar Singh — belong to the shared story of the akhara, and every Indian Olympic wrestling medal stands on its shoulders.

  4. The UK Revival

    Today Sikh akharas across Britain are bringing the culture back — wrestling clubs, gatka championships and community gyms like ours. In 2026, the 12th UK Gatka Championship drew nine akharas from across England.

  5. Khalsa Akhara Coventry

    Since 2013, our gym has carried that tradition in the heart of Foleshill — producing top-ranking wrestlers and boxers, and passing the akhara spirit to the next generation.

ਸੰਤ ਸਿਪਾਹੀ

Sant-sipahi — the saint-soldier. The Sikh ideal that trains strength and humility together, so that power is always in the service of others.

“When you train at Khalsa Akhara, you are stepping into a living tradition — five centuries of the same idea: train the body, steady the mind, hold the community together.” — Khalsa Akhara Gym Coventry
