Who We Are ਅਸੀਂ ਕੌਣ ਹਾਂ

Where Tradition
Meets the Mat

Est. in
Coventry

Khalsa Akhara Wrestling Coventry is the city’s home of kushti — the ancient art of Punjabi wrestling. We welcome wrestlers of every age and experience, from first-timers learning to move, to competitors chasing medals on the national stage.

Our akhara is built on the warrior values of the Khalsa. Every session is coached with discipline and heart — and every member, from our youngest wrestler to our veterans, is held to the same standard.

  • Honesty
  • Integrity
  • Respect
  • Empathy
  • Kindness
  • Humility
  • Compassion

“There is zero tolerance for bullying or discrimination of any kind. The mat is a place where everyone belongs.”

— Khalsa Akhara Wrestling Club
Spotless Mats

Mats are cleaned and disinfected after every single session. Outdoor footwear stays off the mat — always.

Safe & Secure

Kakars securely covered, nails kept short, and full welfare support — your safety comes first.

Real Commitment

Wrestlers commit to 75% attendance — excellence on the mat is built on consistency.
