About Us • Discipline & Respect

Rules of the Akhara

A few simple rules keep the mat safe, clean and respectful for everyone — from our youngest wrestler to our veterans. They’re how we train, how we compete, and how we treat each other.

Home / About Us / Rules of the Akhara

The Basics

General Rules

Every akhara has stood on rules for centuries. Ours are simple — follow them and everyone gets the best of the mat.

1
No shoes on the mat — ever

Outdoor footwear stays outside the wrestling area. Mats are cleaned and disinfected after every single session; a clean mat is a safe mat.

2
No food inside the training area

Eat before or after the session, never on the mats. Water bottles are welcome.

3
Wear the right kit

Comfortable sports clothing with no zips, buttons or sharp edges. No jewellery or watches on the mat.

4
Follow the coach — first time, every time

Coaches keep everyone safe. Instructions are for your safety as much as your progress.

5
Look after the venue

Leave the akhara as you found it — tidy kit, rubbish in the bin, everything respected.

6
Support from the sidelines

Parents and guardians cheer everyone on — coaching is left to the coaches.

Behaviour — On and Off the Mat

Treat everyone with respect — coaches, teammates, opponents and visitors. Win with humility, lose with grace, and always shake hands. No swearing, no bullying, no bad attitudes. The way you behave in the akhara is the way you represent the club everywhere else.

The mat is a place where everyone belongs.

— Khalsa Akhara, zero-tolerance promise

Safety First

Safety & Welfare

Wrestling is a contact sport — these rules keep every wrestler safe, healthy and able to train at their best.

◆
Kakars are securely covered at all times

Kakars must be covered securely and safely before stepping onto the mat — for your protection and everyone else’s.

◆
Infections and open wounds — stay home

Do not train with any infection or open wound. Wrestlers must stay off the mat until fully healed and cleared to return.

◆
Keep nails short

Short fingernails and toenails prevent cuts and scratches for you and your training partners.

◆
Report every injury straight away

Even a small knock. Coaches carry first-aid support at every session and need to know immediately.

◆
Warm up properly

Never skip the warm-up — it’s the foundation of every safe session.

Attendance — the 75% ethos

Wrestlers commit to 75% attendance. Excellence on the mat is built on consistency — every session missed is progress missed, so commit to showing up.

Competitions — the squad pathway

Competition places are earned through attendance, attitude and progress. Coaches select the squad for each event — and every wrestler, at every level, gets a clear path onto the tournament mat.

Your Welfare Officers — Always Here to Help

Gurpreet Singh & H S Dhaliwal are the club’s welfare officers. Any concern — big or small — about safety, wellbeing or anything at all: speak to a welfare officer or any coach. Every concern is taken seriously, and every conversation is handled with care.

Know the Rules.
Live the Values.

That’s all it takes to belong in the akhara. Step onto the mat and our coaches take it from there.
