Before you travel

Dates and venues can change, and entry deadlines are set by the organisers — always check the BWA calendar and the event’s own page before travelling or entering. Wrestlers at the akhara compete through the club: speak to a coach about entering, and remember competition places are earned on the mat through attendance, attitude and progress.
