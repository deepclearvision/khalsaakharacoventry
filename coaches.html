Coach Manny

A lifetime in combat sport, poured into every session at the akhara.

Head Coach — Coach Manny

Coach Manny has been involved in combat sport in one way or another his whole life — starting by developing a love for boxing, where he had an excellent amateur career. His journey next took him to the MMA scene, where he became a sponge and first developed his love for the game of wrestling.

His dedication to the club and its students is huge. Coach Manny’s own children can also be found studying wrestling at the club — and he’s a great person to talk to about any questions around wrestling training.

Every wrestler gets a coach in their corner.

— Khalsa Akhara coaching team

The Team

Our Coaches

Approachable, experienced and always ready to help — on the mat, on competition day, and everywhere in between.

Coach Dhaliwal

Coach Dhaliwal’s love for wrestling started in the Punjab and followed him to the UK. Alongside coaching, he keeps a keen eye on the developing wrestling scene in India. Coach Dhaliwal’s children also attend the club.

At the club he’s definitely a person you can approach with any issues — he will go over and above to solve them.

Punjab roots Welfare officer

Coach Khosa

Coach Khosa is the newest member of the coaching team and a very powerful asset to the club. His wrestling journey started in Wolverhampton, where he studied and learned the art at a grassroots level.

He has been a tremendous help during mat sessions and on competition days — where he has been known to keep the team fed with some very good subs.

Grassroots wrestling Competition days

Coach Gurpreet

Coach Gurpreet has completely transformed the admin side of the club, making it a much smoother-running operation. He’s quick to respond to parent issues and anything wrestlers may be struggling with — the link between the different parts of the club.

Gurpreet also helps on the mats and gets the message out about what the club has been up to through social media. A real pleasure to be around.
