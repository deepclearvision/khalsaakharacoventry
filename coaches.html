Head Coach • Level 2 • DBS Checked

Mani Sandhu

Mani’s journey in combat sports began at a young age with amateur boxing, competing in numerous bouts and developing a strong foundation in striking. A later transition into MMA opened Mani’s eyes to the importance of wrestling — excellent boxing skills mean little if you cannot defend a takedown or control where a fight takes place. Wrestling is the foundation that dictates where a fight goes.

That understanding sparked a passion for wrestling that ultimately led to coaching — developing and passing on the skills and values of the sport. Mani believes wrestling offers children far more than just a sport: the complex movements involved help develop coordination, balance, body awareness and brain development. Constantly thinking, reacting and adapting to an opponent encourages problem-solving, concentration and decision-making. Just as importantly, wrestling builds confidence, discipline, emotional control and resilience. It teaches the value of hard work, how to deal with challenges, how to overcome setbacks and, above all, to never give up — life skills that extend far beyond the wrestling mat.
